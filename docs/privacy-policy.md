# Sokoban: privacy policy

*Last updated: 2 October 2026*

Sokoban (`dev.stefan.sokoban`) does not collect, store on a server, or share
any personal or usage data.

- The app has no internet access: it does not request the network permission,
  and contains no ads, analytics, crash reporting or account system.
- Your progress (solved levels, best move counts) and your settings (sound,
  vibration, theme) are kept in the app's private storage on your device.
- If Android backup is turned on, that same progress and those settings are
  included in your device's backup and restored with it. Backups are handled
  by Android and your Google account; the developer has no access to them.
- The only permission the app uses is vibration, for haptic feedback while
  playing. It can be switched off in the app's settings.

Uninstalling the app, or using **Settings → Reset progress** in the app,
erases the progress stored on the device.
