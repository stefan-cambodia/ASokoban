Push every crate onto a target. Simple rules, real puzzles.

Sokoban is the classic warehouse puzzle: you can push one crate at a time and never pull one back, so every move counts. Plan ahead, or get stuck.

• 70 hand-designed levels in seven worlds, from First Steps to the Labyrinth
• Every level verified solvable
• Unlimited undo, and a restart for a fresh look
• Up to three stars for solving a level within par
• Solving a level opens the next two, so one hard puzzle never blocks you
• Notices when a crate can no longer reach a target and suggests an undo
• Play with the on-screen pad, swipes, tap-to-walk or a keyboard
• Light and dark themes
• Sound effects and haptic feedback, each of which can be switched off
• Screen-reader descriptions of the board

No ads, no in-app purchases, no account. The game has no internet access and collects nothing: your progress stays on your device and in your own Android backup.
